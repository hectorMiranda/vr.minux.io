# Snap Turn

**Category:** Locomotion &middot; **XR:** vr &middot; **Tags:** `locomotion`, `snap`, `turn`, `rotation`

Pillars arranged in a circle; snap-turn rotates the view 30° per flick with a compass heading indicator.

## Run it

Open the launcher and pick **Snap Turn**, or deep-link straight to it:

```
http://localhost:5173/#snap-turn-demo
```

## What this verifies

Verifies a locomotion technique and its comfort behaviour by moving the camera rig rather than the camera.
