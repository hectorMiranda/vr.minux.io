# Elevator Platform

**Category:** Locomotion &middot; **XR:** vr &middot; **Tags:** `locomotion`, `platform`, `elevator`, `vertical`

Stand on a platform that automatically rises and lowers between floors; the rig tracks the platform.

## Run it

Open the launcher and pick **Elevator Platform**, or deep-link straight to it:

```
http://localhost:5173/#elevator-platform
```

## What this verifies

Verifies a locomotion technique and its comfort behaviour by moving the camera rig rather than the camera.
