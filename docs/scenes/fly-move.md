# Fly Move

**Category:** Locomotion &middot; **XR:** vr &middot; **Tags:** `locomotion`, `fly`, `6dof`, `controller`

Free 6DoF flight controlled by controller orientation and trigger; auto-flies through a ring course on desktop.

## Run it

Open the launcher and pick **Fly Move**, or deep-link straight to it:

```
http://localhost:5173/#fly-move
```

## What this verifies

Verifies a locomotion technique and its comfort behaviour by moving the camera rig rather than the camera.
